A Shepherd's Heart

“It is my joy to welcome you to Jesus Celebration Centre. Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or seeking a church to call home, you belong here. Our prayer is that you encounter the love of Jesus and discover the purpose God has placed within you.”

Our Senior Pastor

Our Senior Pastor has faithfully served Jesus Celebration Centre – Kitengela Town for over two decades, alongside a dedicated family committed to the ministry of the Gospel.

With a heart for worship and discipleship, the vision has always been simple yet profound: to see ordinary people transformed into Kingdom Ambassadors who impact their homes, workplaces and communities for Christ.

Beyond the pulpit, our pastor is passionate about mentorship, marriage and family enrichment, and compassionate outreach to the wider Kitengela community.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with God for years, you will find a shepherd's heart ready to welcome, encourage and pray with you.
